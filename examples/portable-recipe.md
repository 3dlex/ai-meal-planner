# Pan-Seared Chicken Thighs with Tomato-Corn Salad & Herbed Quinoa

**Servings:** 2  
**Total time:** 35 minutes  
**Estimated calories:** 580–630 per serving  
**Style:** American / Summer

## Ingredients

- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (~1.5 lb)
- 1 cup quinoa
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 ears fresh corn, kernels cut off cob
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, finely diced
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Rinse the quinoa. Bring the broth to a boil, add quinoa, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes.
2. Fluff the quinoa and stir in half the parsley, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Pat the chicken dry and season with smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
5. Sear chicken skin-side down for 6–7 minutes. Flip and cook another 8–10 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
6. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes.
7. Combine corn, tomatoes, red onion, basil, remaining parsley, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
8. Serve the chicken with the quinoa and tomato-corn salad.

## Optional Prep Ahead

- Cook the quinoa in advance and reheat.
- Prepare the tomato-corn salad earlier in the day.
