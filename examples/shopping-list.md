# Shopping List

This shopping list represents the combined ingredients required for the recipes selected for export.

The user may export:

- one recipe
- several selected recipes
- the complete weekly plan

Only ingredients required by the selected recipes should appear in this list.

Duplicate ingredients should be combined into practical purchase quantities whenever possible.

## Notes

- Pantry staples should normally be omitted unless they are unusual or recipe-specific.
- Quantities should reflect the combined needs of the selected recipes.
- The shopping list should remain one consolidated checklist even when multiple recipe files are exported.
- When all seven recipes are exported, this becomes the shopping list for the complete weekly plan.
