# Weekly Dinner Plan

## Monday — Lemon Herb Salmon with Roasted Broccoli

**Cuisine or style:** Mediterranean-inspired
**Estimated total time:** 35 minutes
**Estimated calories:** 500–560 per serving

### Ingredients

* 2 salmon fillets, about 6 oz each
* 1 large broccoli crown, cut into florets
* 1 lemon
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* 1 cup cooked brown rice

### Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the broccoli with 1 tablespoon olive oil and arrange it on a baking sheet.
3. Roast for 10 minutes.
4. Brush the salmon with the remaining olive oil and season with garlic, oregano, and lemon.
5. Add the salmon to the baking sheet and roast for another 12 to 15 minutes.
6. Serve with brown rice and lemon wedges.

### Optional Prep Ahead

* Cut the broccoli into florets.
* Cook the brown rice in advance and reheat before serving.

## Tuesday — Chicken Fajita Bowls

**Cuisine or style:** Tex-Mex
**Estimated total time:** 35 minutes
**Estimated calories:** 550–620 per serving

### Ingredients

* 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced
* 1 bell pepper, sliced
* 1 small onion, sliced
* 1 cup cooked brown rice
* 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
* 1 lime
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp chili powder
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
* Fresh cilantro, optional

### Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the chicken, cumin, and chili powder and cook until lightly browned.
3. Add the pepper and onion and cook until tender and the chicken is cooked through.
4. Warm the black beans and brown rice.
5. Divide the rice, beans, chicken, and vegetables between two bowls.
6. Top with Greek yogurt, lime juice, and cilantro if desired.

### Optional Prep Ahead

* Slice the chicken and vegetables.
* Cook extra brown rice while preparing Monday's dinner.

## Wednesday — Mediterranean Chickpea Pasta

**Cuisine or style:** Mediterranean
**Estimated total time:** 30 minutes
**Estimated calories:** 520–590 per serving

### Ingredients

* 6 oz whole-wheat pasta
* 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
* 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
* 2 cups baby spinach
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 lemon
* 2 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1/2 tsp dried oregano

### Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to package directions.
2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
3. Add garlic and cherry tomatoes and cook until the tomatoes begin to soften.
4. Stir in the chickpeas, spinach, and oregano.
5. Add the drained pasta and toss until the spinach has wilted.
6. Finish with lemon juice and Parmesan.

### Optional Prep Ahead

* Halve the cherry tomatoes.
* Rinse and drain the chickpeas.

## Thursday — Turkey and Vegetable Stir-Fry

**Cuisine or style:** Asian-inspired
**Estimated total time:** 30 minutes
**Estimated calories:** 500–570 per serving

### Ingredients

* 12 oz lean ground turkey
* 1 broccoli crown, cut into small florets
* 1 bell pepper, sliced
* 1 small carrot, thinly sliced
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
* 2 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
* 1 tsp honey
* 1 tsp sesame oil
* 1 cup cooked brown rice

### Instructions

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the turkey and cook until browned.
3. Add the broccoli, pepper, carrot, garlic, and ginger.
4. Stir-fry until the vegetables are crisp-tender.
5. Stir together the soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil.
6. Add the sauce to the skillet and toss to coat.
7. Serve over brown rice.

### Optional Prep Ahead

* Cut the vegetables in advance.
* Cook the rice earlier in the week and refrigerate it.

## Friday — Sheet-Pan Sausage, Sweet Potato, and Green Beans

**Cuisine or style:** American weeknight
**Estimated total time:** 40 minutes
**Estimated calories:** 560–630 per serving

### Ingredients

* 8 oz chicken or turkey sausage, sliced
* 1 large sweet potato, cut into small cubes
* 8 oz green beans, trimmed
* 1 small onion, cut into wedges
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp smoked paprika
* 1/2 tsp garlic powder

### Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the sweet potato and onion with half the olive oil and seasonings.
3. Roast for 15 minutes.
4. Add the sausage and green beans to the pan.
5. Toss with the remaining oil.
6. Roast for another 15 to 20 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the sausage is heated through.

### Optional Prep Ahead

* Cube the sweet potato.
* Trim the green beans.

## Saturday — Garlic-Lime Shrimp Tacos with Cabbage Slaw

**Cuisine or style:** Mexican-inspired
**Estimated total time:** 25 minutes
**Estimated calories:** 480–550 per serving

### Ingredients

* 12 oz peeled and deveined shrimp
* 6 small corn tortillas
* 2 cups shredded cabbage
* 1 lime
* 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
* 1 clove garlic, minced
* 1/2 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* Fresh cilantro, optional

### Instructions

1. Mix the cabbage with half the lime juice and Greek yogurt.
2. Season the shrimp with garlic and cumin.
3. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Cook the shrimp for 2 to 3 minutes per side until opaque.
5. Warm the tortillas.
6. Fill each tortilla with shrimp and cabbage slaw.
7. Finish with the remaining lime juice and cilantro if desired.

### Optional Prep Ahead

* Prepare the cabbage slaw several hours ahead and refrigerate.

## Sunday — Chicken, Spinach, and White Bean Skillet

**Cuisine or style:** Rustic Italian-inspired
**Estimated total time:** 35 minutes
**Estimated calories:** 520–590 per serving

### Ingredients

* 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
* 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
* 2 cups baby spinach
* 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1/2 small onion, diced
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1/2 tsp dried oregano
* 2 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese
* 2 slices whole-grain bread

### Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
3. Add the onion and garlic and cook until softened.
4. Stir in the tomatoes, cannellini beans, and oregano.
5. Cook until the chicken is fully cooked and the beans are heated through.
6. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
7. Sprinkle with Parmesan and serve with whole-grain bread.

### Optional Prep Ahead

* Dice the onion and halve the tomatoes.
* Drain and rinse the beans.

# Weekly Prep Opportunities

Several ingredients can be prepared ahead without making advance preparation necessary.

* Cook a larger batch of brown rice early in the week for Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday.
* Cut both broccoli crowns at the same time.
* Slice the bell peppers and onions for Tuesday and Thursday together.
* Wash and portion the spinach for Wednesday and Sunday.
* Halve the cherry tomatoes for Wednesday and Sunday.
* Trim the green beans and cube the sweet potato for Friday.
* Shred the cabbage for Saturday if not purchased pre-shredded.
* Mince several cloves of garlic at once and refrigerate them in a covered container.

These steps are optional. Each meal is still intended to remain within the stated cooking-time limit without advance preparation.
